PBIX is not direct input. “Safe” applies to supported scanned metadata; dynamic or unsupported metadata remains limited/Review. BOM-prefixed TMDL scopes are read-only for saved changes. These boundaries are reflected in the [candidate notes](../../releases/windows-candidate.md), [quick start](../../quickstart.md), and [support guidance](../../support.md).
