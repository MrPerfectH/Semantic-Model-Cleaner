Proposal 2

Split Workspace

This version treats Details as an analysis workspace, not just a data table. Items are grouped by usage source, the inspector is stronger, and the page feels more like a guided review surface. It explains more, but it also asks more from the user.

Best for beta Grouped by meaning High explainability

Why this direction

Strongest if you want the product to feel more premium and less spreadsheet-like. Risk: it may become too heavy for very large models and needs careful simplification later.

  • Back to comparisonReturn to the proposal index.
Intent: the user should not have to mentally translate `USED` into three different meanings. The page itself becomes the explanation: report-driven evidence, model-only retention, and cleanup candidates live in separate visual zones.
