Report Health

These problems live in the report files (PBIR), not the semantic model: references to measures that no longer exist, stale formatting selectors, invalid JSON, and metadata the analyzer cannot fully verify. They matter because they downgrade Cleanup recommendations to Review and can make Usage look stronger or weaker than what report authors actually see. The repair actions here fix the report files.
